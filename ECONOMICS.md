# ECONOMICS.md

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Based on publicly available information about AI credit markets:

- Average deal size: ~$2,000–$8,000 (discounted credit packages for teams of 5–50)
- Assume conservative average: **$3,500 per closed deal**
- Gross margin on credits: ~25–35% (buying at discount, selling below retail)
- Gross profit per deal: **~$875–$1,225**
- Use $1,000 as the working number

A "converted lead" = audit completed → Credex consultation booked → credit purchase.

## Conversion funnel assumptions

| Stage | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor → audit completed | 20% | No login = lower friction |
| Audit completed → email captured | 35% | Value shown first |
| Email captured → consultation booked | 8% | High-savings filter helps |
| Consultation → credit purchase | 40% | Credex existing close rate |

**Overall: visitor → purchase = 0.20 × 0.35 × 0.08 × 0.40 = 0.22%**

To get 10 purchases: need ~4,500 visitors.

## Break-even and $1M ARR

**Revenue per visitor:** $3,500 × 0.0022 = **$7.70**

**$1M ARR math:**
- Need ~285 closed deals/year (~24/month)
- At 40% close rate on consultations: need ~715 consultations/year (~60/month)
- At 8% audit→consultation rate: need ~8,900 email captures/year (~740/month)
- At 35% email capture rate: need ~25,400 completed audits/year (~2,100/month)
- At 20% completion rate: need **~127,000 visitors/year (~10,600/month)**

**What would have to be true:**
1. SpendLens gets 10,000+ visitors/month — achievable with consistent HN/Twitter presence + SEO from the blog post bonus feature
2. The high-savings filter works — only surfacing Credex to users with >$500/mo savings keeps consultation quality high and close rate at 40%
3. Credex builds a lightweight CRM to follow up on high-savings leads within 24 hours — speed matters
4. Pricing data stays current — if the audit is wrong, word spreads fast

**Conservative 18-month scenario:** 50 closed deals total, $175,000 in revenue. Achievable with the existing Credex list + one viral HN moment.

**Optimistic 18-month scenario:** 300 closed deals, $1.05M revenue. Requires sustained distribution and the benchmark mode feature driving repeat visits.
