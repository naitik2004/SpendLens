## FAQ — 5 real Q&As

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes, completely. No credit card, no account, no trial. SpendLens is a free tool built by Credex. We make money when teams with significant savings buy discounted AI credits from us — but only if they choose to.

**Q: How is the savings number calculated?**
A: By comparing your current plan against official vendor pricing for the plan that actually fits your team size and use case. Every recommendation cites a specific reason — we don't manufacture savings that aren't there. If your stack is optimal, we'll tell you that too.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: Your audit result (tools, plans, savings numbers) is stored to generate your shareable URL. Your email, if you provide it, is stored separately and never sold. Company name and role are optional.

**Q: What's Credex?**
A: Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise — from companies that over-purchased or pivoted. For teams with significant savings opportunities, Credex can often go further than plan changes alone.

**Q: Why should I trust the pricing data?**
A: Every price in the audit traces to an official vendor pricing page URL, verified the week of publication. We publish all sources in our open GitHub repo. If you spot an error, open an issue — we'll fix it within 24 hours.
